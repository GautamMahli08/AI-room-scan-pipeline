Record following the video box in docs/CAPTURE_PROTOCOL.md:
Use the normal Camera app in Video mode, 1× lens.
Walk slowly along the walls, and tilt up to the ceiling once per room.
Walk through the doorways and finish where you started.
Keep the phone the same way up throughout.
Copy the file to the computer, for example to F:\LiDAR\captures\myroom.mp4. iPhone .MOV files work too.
Run, in a terminal in F:\LiDAR:

bin\scan.exe run captures\myroom.mp4
The video tier is picked automatically from the file type.
Open the result: results\myroom_video\plan.svg. Add -debug to the command if you also want the coloured picture.
What to expect:

Time	About 15 s of processing per second of video on your laptop (a 30 s video takes about 7–9 min)
Rotation	Automatic. Phone videos store their orientation, and sideways videos are also detected
Accuracy	Rough, as the docs say: rooms come out as estimated rectangles, often too small, with wide ranges. Doors usually aren't found
Second run	Instant, because the AI results are saved. Use -live to force a fresh run
Photos work the same way. Make one folder per room inside one main folder (e.g. captures\myflat\kitchen\, captures\myflat\bedroom\) with 2–8 photos each, then run:


bin\scan.exe run captures\myflat -tier photo
For a real accuracy test, measure too. Measure 3–4 walls with a tape measure and compare with the plan. That would be the first real ground truth for the video and photo tiers.
